Layer settings

Reading it

  • For cuts, the winner is the fastest cell that pushes out cleanly with the least char on the back face.
  • For engraving, judge under the light the finished part will live in. Contrast matters more than depth.
  • Write the winner on the offcut with the date and the sheet's supplier, then save it as a recipe.
  • Run it again after a lens clean or a new sheet supplier. Plywood varies more than people expect.

Never in the laser

Chlorinated and fluorinated plastics give off acid gas that ruins the machine and hurts people. No label, no SDS, no cutting.

What the pantry holds

Cut depths assume a healthy machine. "Mark" means surface only — no through-cutting, ever.

House rules

    Every recipe here is a starting point. The sheet in front of you is the final authority.
